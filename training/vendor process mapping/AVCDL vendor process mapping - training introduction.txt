This training covers how to map a supplier's cybersecurity processes to AVCDL processes.

You will learn:
* The importance of mapping a supplier's cybersecurity lifecycle to AVCDL
* How the mapping supports requirements tailoring and CIA definition
* How to use an existing template to map a lifecycle to AVCDL and evaluate the results
* How to address process gaps that exist between a supplier's cybersecurity lifecycle and AVCDL
